Sprunki Italian Brainrot

Mine Blocks is a 2D Minecraft fan action game originally released in Flash. You can now play the game again, as it's been rebuilt in HTML5 and features regular updates from the development team. Explore, craft, and create a world of your imaginings in this open-world playground!

Controls

Click and hold any clothing item or accessory, then drag it onto your Sprunki to dress them.
